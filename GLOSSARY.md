# Glossary

- **Walk**: the scroll-driven pixel-art game at the top of the page.
- **Track**: the scroll distance of the walk. It maps a scroll fraction to a world position, walk progress, and the finale.
- **World position**: a horizontal place in the walk, in logical pixels.
- **Finale**: the last part of the track, after the walk ends, when the door opens and the view moves into the cabin.
- **Stop**: one employer's place in the walk: its landmark, zone, gems, and timeline mark. One stop can hold several jobs.
- **Job**: one role on the résumé, with its own id.
- **Landmark**: a building or prop sprite placed in the world. Anchors are points on a landmark.
- **Zone**: the stretch of the walk where a stop's card shows.
- **Gem**: a collectible skill.
- **Toolkit**: the collected gems.
- **Sprint**: Dan catching up to a fast scroll by running faster. He never skips.
- **Snap**: moving Dan with no travel. Only teleport, reduced motion, and the first frame snap.
- **Teleport**: the beam effect for timeline jumps, which hides a snap.
- **Pixel dissolve**: the transition for links to page sections.
- **Tailored version**: a résumé variant that overrides bullets and skills by job id.
- **Room**: the cabin interior. There is a wide layout and a tall layout for narrow screens.
- **Terminal**: the toy Rex screen on the room's monitor. Visitors can type on it; it only prints canned replies.
